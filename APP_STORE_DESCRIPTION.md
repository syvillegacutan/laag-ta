# App Store Description — Laag Ta!

**App Name:** Laag Ta!  
**Subtitle:** Your travel companion  
**Category:** Travel  
**Age Rating:** 4+

---

## App Store Description

**Laag Ta! — Your Adventure Starts Here ✈️**

*"Laag ta" means "Let's go explore!" in Bisaya — the language of millions of Filipinos from Mindanao and the Visayas. Born in Cagayan de Oro, Philippines, this app carries that same spirit of joyful wandering wherever your passport takes you.*

Whether you're navigating the alleyways of Tokyo, haggling in a Bangkok market, or ordering coffee in Paris without the deer-in-headlights face — Laag Ta! has your back.

---

**✨ TRANSLATE ANYTHING, INSTANTLY**
Point your keyboard (or your voice!) at any language barrier. Powered by Claude AI, Laag Ta! doesn't just translate — it gives you the pronunciation too, so you can actually *say* it. Hit Listen to hear it spoken aloud, Copy to paste it anywhere, or Save it to your personal phrasebook for later.

**💬 OFFLINE PHRASES THAT ACTUALLY HELP**
No WiFi? No problem. Over 300 hand-picked travel phrases across 6 essential categories — Greetings, Food, Transport, Emergency, Shopping, and Hotel — available for 8 languages, ready to use even in airplane mode. Tap any phrase to hear it spoken in the right accent.

**📍 FIND AMAZING PLACES NEARBY**
Your next great meal is probably 200 meters away. Laag Ta! uses your location to surface restaurants, hotels, cafes, pharmacies, ATMs, and tourist spots on a live map. Tap any pin or card to get instant directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps.

**💸 NEVER GET CONFUSED BY MONEY AGAIN**
Live exchange rates for 10 currencies — PHP, USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, JPY, KRW, VND, THB, and SGD. Type any amount in any currency and see everything converted at once. Rates refresh on every app open, with offline fallback so you're never in the dark.

---

**WHY LAAG TA!?**

Syville Gacutan, a solo designer-developer from CDO, built this app the way she'd want to travel: fearless, well-prepared, and a little bit Bisaya. Every screen is designed to get out of your way and let the adventure in.

*Dali, adventure awaits!*

---

**LANGUAGES SUPPORTED:** Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Spanish, French, German, Filipino, and more via AI translation.

**WORKS OFFLINE:** Phrases, cached exchange rates.

**PRIVACY:** Location data stays on your device. We never sell your data.
