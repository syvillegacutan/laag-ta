## Full Description

**Laag Ta! — Because Every Adventure Deserves a Good Companion ✈️**

You know that moment when you're somewhere amazing — a street food stall in Hanoi, a ramen shop in Fukuoka, a hostel check-in desk in Barcelona — and you just wish you could speak the language? That's exactly why Laag Ta! exists.

*"Laag ta" is Bisaya (Cebuano) for "Let's go explore!" — a phrase from the lips of millions in Mindanao and the Visayas, Philippines. This app was built in Cagayan de Oro by Syville Gacutan, who believes that a language barrier should never keep you from the best experiences life has to offer.*

---

🌐 **AI TRANSLATION THAT SOUNDS HUMAN**
Forget robotic, word-for-word translations. Laag Ta! uses Claude AI to translate naturally — and gives you the romanized pronunciation so you can say it out loud with confidence. Type your phrase, hit Translate, and get the words AND how to say them. Then hit Listen, and the app speaks it for you. You can Copy the translation or Save it to your personal phrasebook.

📚 **OFFLINE PHRASEBOOK — NO INTERNET NEEDED**
Pick your destination language, pick a category — Greetings, Food, Transport, Emergency, Shopping, or Hotel — and you've got the right words ready to go, even on airplane mode. 300+ curated phrases across 8 languages. Tap any phrase to hear it pronounced aloud.

🗺️ **NEARBY PLACES ON A LIVE MAP**
Running low on cash? Hungry? Need a pharmacy? Laag Ta! finds what you need around you — restaurants, hotels, ATMs, cafes, tourist spots, convenience stores — and shows them on a map with real ratings. Tap a card and it opens Google Maps directions automatically.

💰 **LIVE CURRENCY CONVERTER**
Ten currencies in one glance: PHP 🇵🇭, USD 🇺🇸, EUR 🇪🇺, GBP 🇬🇧, AUD 🇦🇺, JPY 🇯🇵, KRW 🇰🇷, VND 🇻🇳, THB 🇹🇭, SGD 🇸🇬. Type any amount, switch the base currency, and watch everything update instantly. Rates are live and cached offline so you always have a fallback.

---

**THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME**

"Laag" in Bisaya/Cebuano means to wander, roam, or go around exploring. It's what you do when you're not rushing anywhere — just taking in the world. "Ta" means "we" or "let's." Put them together and you have the most Filipino way of saying: let's go somewhere amazing.

Syville Gacutan, a solo developer and designer from Cagayan de Oro, built Laag Ta! as a love letter to every Filipino traveler who's ever gone abroad wondering if they'd be okay. *Gihimo nako ni para sa atong mga maghilaagay!* (Made this for all of us who love to explore!)
